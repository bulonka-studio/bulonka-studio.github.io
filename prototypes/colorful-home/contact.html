What to include in your first email

Helps the studio give you a useful answer instead of asking three rounds of clarifying questions.

  1. What the app is for, and who it's for.
  2. Where you are today (idea, wireframes, or an existing app to audit).
  3. Any privacy or regulatory constraints (GDPR, HIPAA, etc.).
  4. Rough timeline and budget if you have them.
